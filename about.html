About SkillNest

We turn curiosity into practical learning for students and freshers.

SkillNest is based in Pune, Maharashtra and is built for college students, early professionals, freshers, and knowledge seekers who want practical technology learning that feels relevant and useful.

Our mission

Help learners become future-ready with confidence and direction.

We create practical training experiences in Cloud, Data Analysis, AI, and Cybersecurity so learners can build confidence, improve career readiness, and keep growing through workshops and guided learning experiences.
